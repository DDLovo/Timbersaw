# Changelog

## 0.2.0

- Fixed Octarine Core failing to reduce skill cooldowns with the current R2API.

- Added the complete Molten Destructor skin, close-up survivor portraits, and transparent status icons.
- Rebuilt Timber Chain with solid 3D links, 30 m/s base pull speed, full movement-speed scaling for range and 50% bonus scaling for pull speed, and more forgiving hook collision.
- Timber Chain now retains more momentum when the pull ends for a smoother follow-through.
- Flamethrower and Chakram slows now affect bosses. Shiva's Guard still only slows non-boss enemies.
- Jetpack now starts at sprint speed, accelerates to 200% bonus speed, and has an 18s cooldown.
- Removed Exposure Therapy's extra-target damage cap.
- Simplified Twisted Chakram aiming and fixed Chakram appearing washed out in fog.
- Consolidated Chakram slow into one debuff and added distinct native-style icons for Chakram and Shiva slows.
- Shortened skill descriptions in English and Simplified Chinese; detailed rules appear in keyword tooltips.

## 0.1.3

- R aiming now shows Chakram's full cylindrical damage volume, including its height.
- Enemies inside the preview are highlighted using the game's outline effect. Targets behind walls are excluded.

## 0.1.2

- Made the spinning Chakram center less transparent.
- Added a filled damage-area preview at the R destination, including Shiva's Guard's increased radius.


## 0.1.1

- Rebuilt Chakram with a textured solid model visible from both sides; reduced its radius to 6m.
- Hold R to aim, release to launch, and press again to recall. Simplified the targeting indicator.
- Reworked Whirling Death and Exposure Therapy effects and kept them horizontal.
- Removed the oversized armor plates. Jetpack now accelerates from 0% to 100% bonus speed.


## 0.1.0

- Added Timbersaw with eight skills and three exclusive relics.
- Included the classic model, animations, skill sounds, and Dota-inspired visual effects.
- Added three-dimensional Chakram paths, range indicators, and thick damage volumes.
- Added English and Simplified Chinese text, configurable sound volume, and LookingGlass proc information.
